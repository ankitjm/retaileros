Multi-store

Running more than one store?

Yes — RetailerOS runs multiple stores from one login. Each location keeps its own stock, staff, cash register and invoice numbering, while you get a single owner's view across all of them. The Chain plan covers 2 stores at ₹7,499/month, with additional stores at ₹3,499 per store per month — and every store brings 3 staff logins with it, at no extra charge. Consolidating always costs less than running separate accounts.

One dashboard, every location

Today's revenue, tasks and quick actions across all your stores on a single executive home screen — instead of phoning each counter at closing time.

Stock held per location

Brands, product lists, stock levels and inward/outward movement are tracked separately for each store, so you always know what is sitting where.

Approve discounts remotely

Dynamic pricing rules protect your margin, and a staff member at any counter can raise a discount request for you to approve from wherever you are.

Staff, shifts and commission per store

Employee profiles, attendance, shifts and commission tracking are kept per location, with configurable team roles set store by store.

Separate cash close per counter

Each store opens its own shifts, closes its own day and reconciles its own collections — so a mismatch is traceable to one counter, not the whole business.

Store-level invoice settings

Taxes, invoice numbering series, print templates and team roles are configured per store, keeping each location's GST paperwork clean and independent.

Compare store performance

Sales trends, staff leaderboards and scheme ROI dashboards let you see which location is pulling ahead and which one needs attention this month.

Search an IMEI across every store

Find any handset's full chain of custody no matter which location took it in — so a customer walking into the wrong branch is still your customer.

How many stores can I manage on one account?

Free and Shop each cover one store. Chain includes two, and you can add as many more as you like at ₹3,499 per store per month. Running a larger chain? Talk to us about a custom rollout.

Do I pay extra for staff logins when I open a store?

No. Every store you add includes 3 staff logins automatically — a 15-store group gets 45 logins at no extra charge. You only pay per login if a single store needs more than three.

Do I need a separate login for each store?

No. One owner login reaches every location. Staff are given their own accounts with roles configured per store, so a counter assistant sees only what they need to.

Can each store have its own invoice numbering?

Yes. Taxes, invoice numbering series and print templates are store-level settings, so each location issues its own clean GST-compliant series.

Can I find a handset if another branch sold it?

Cross-store IMEI search comes with Chain. On Free and Shop, IMEI chain-of-custody tracking works fully within your single store. Compare the plans →

Questions & Answers

Before you decide.

  1. Q1

    Are taxes included in these prices?

    All listed prices exclude 18% GST per Indian regulations. Every invoice shows base price and GST clearly, ready to file.

  2. Q2

    Can I cancel any time?

    Yes — from your account settings, any time. Your plan stays active until the end of the billing period. No charge for the next cycle.

  3. Q3

    Can I switch plans mid-cycle?

    Upgrades take effect immediately and we pro-rate the difference. Downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing period.

  4. Q4

    What if I exceed my plan's bill limit?

    We won't block your billing. You'll see a friendly prompt to upgrade or buy add-ons. No surprise overage charges.

  5. Q5

    Do you offer refunds?

    Quarterly and annual plans get a pro-rated refund within the first 30 days, no questions asked. Email us from your registered address.

  6. Q6

    What happens after the 14-day trial?

    Add a payment method to continue on the paid plan, or your account drops to Free. Either way, your data stays intact.
