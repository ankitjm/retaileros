The full system

Twenty-three modules. One platform.

Every part of running a mobile retail shop, in one place — switchable without context loss.

  • Sales DeskCore POS billing — create bills, scan IMEIs, process payments, print receipts.
  • SchemesBrand and bank offer calculator that surfaces applicable discounts and EMIs during billing.
  • PricingMargin protection — dynamic pricing rules and remote discount approvals.
  • InvoicesGST-compliant document generator for tax invoices, credit notes, and proformas.
  • InventoryStock management — brands, product lists, stock levels, inward/outward movement.
  • IMEI TrackerDedicated search and full chain-of-custody tracking for individual device lifecycles.
  • Purchase OrdersProcurement workflow — vendor orders and Goods Received Notes (GRN).
  • StoresMulti-store management — oversee inventory and performance across locations.
  • Clients (CRM)Customer relationship management — directories, purchase history, credit tracking.
  • MarketingBroadcast WhatsApp/SMS campaigns and build trigger-based automations.
  • Help / InquiriesWalk-in lead management — track customer requests, follow-ups, pre-booking deposits.
  • RepairsJob card management — device intake through diagnosis, repair, customer delivery.
  • ClaimsWorkflow tracker for managing brand warranty claims from filing to settlement.
  • Cash RegisterDaily cash management — open shifts, close days, reconcile collections.
  • ExpensesDaily expense tracker and monthly budget management — replaces paper diaries.
  • StaffTeam management — employee profiles, attendance, shifts, commission tracking.
  • FinanceAccountant-facing day books, cash flow, ledgers, and GST return summaries.
  • ReportsBusiness intelligence — sales trends, staff leaderboards, scheme ROI dashboards.
  • Auth / OnboardingLogin, registration gateway, and store setup wizard.
  • Launcher (Dashboard)Executive home — today's revenue, tasks, quick actions.
  • SettingsStore-level configurations — taxes, invoice numbering, print templates, team roles.
  • IntegrationsConnect Tally, Busy, WATI, delivery partners and other third-party tools.
  • MarketplaceTwo-sided hub — B2B retailer trading board and a consumer-facing storefront.

Questions & Answers

Before you decide.

  1. Q1

    Are taxes included in these prices?

    All listed prices exclude 18% GST per Indian regulations. Every invoice shows base price and GST clearly, ready to file.

  2. Q2

    Can I cancel any time?

    Yes — from your account settings, any time. Your plan stays active until the end of the billing period. No charge for the next cycle.

  3. Q3

    Can I switch plans mid-cycle?

    Upgrades take effect immediately and we pro-rate the difference. Downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing period.

  4. Q4

    What if I exceed my plan's bill limit?

    We won't block your billing. You'll see a friendly prompt to upgrade or buy add-ons. No surprise overage charges.

  5. Q5

    Do you offer refunds?

    Quarterly and annual plans get a pro-rated refund within the first 30 days, no questions asked. Email us from your registered address.

  6. Q6

    What happens after the 14-day trial?

    Add a payment method to continue on the paid plan, or your account drops to Starter Free. Either way, your data stays intact.
