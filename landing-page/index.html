The problem

Every electronics counter has four leaks.

None of them shows up on the day's sales figure. All of them come out of your margin. Generic billing software prints a GST invoice and leaves every one of them open.

LEAK 01

Scheme money you never claimed

Brand and bank schemes change every week. The claim you forgot to file is margin you already earned — gone.

THE FIXSchemes apply at billing, and the claim is queued and tracked until the brand pays.
LEAK 02

The unit you can't trace

A phone, AC or TV sold without its IMEI or serial logged means a warranty fight, a theft you can't prove and an audit that doesn't match.

THE FIXEvery unit is tracked by IMEI or serial from purchase to sale to warranty.
LEAK 03

Khaata that quietly ages

Credit given at the counter, written in a notebook, chased on WhatsApp — or not chased at all.

THE FIXA running ledger for every customer, updated by every bill and payment.
LEAK 04

The 9 PM close

Matching cash, UPI, card and bills by hand every night, long after the shutter is down.

THE FIXStock, cash register and reports agree as you go. Close the day in minutes.

Questions

Before you decide.

What is RetailerOS?

RetailerOS is retail management software for Indian consumer electronics stores — mobiles, appliances, ACs, TVs and laptops. It runs GST billing, IMEI and serial tracking, brand scheme claims, repairs, customer khaata, staff and reports in one system.

Is the Free plan really free?

Yes. Free covers one store with 3 staff logins, 50 bills a month and 7 modules, including the Sales Desk, invoices, inventory and customer records. No card is needed and there is no time limit.

How long does it take to set up?

About 10 minutes for your first counter. If you are moving from Tally, Vyapar or spreadsheets, we bring your inventory and khaata over for you.

Do I have to stop using Tally?

Not for your books. RetailerOS runs the retail counter: billing, stock, IMEI and serial tracking, schemes, warranty claims and repairs. Many retailers keep their accountant on Tally for filing-grade accounts.

How many stores can I manage on one RetailerOS account?

Free and Shop each cover one store. Chain needs a minimum of two stores: the first store is Rs 7,499 per month and every additional store is Rs 3,499 per month. There is no cap on how many stores a Chain account can run.

Do I pay extra for staff logins when I open a new store?

No. Every store you add includes 3 staff logins automatically, so a 15-store group gets 45 logins at no extra charge. You only pay per login if a single store needs more than three.

Are taxes included in these prices?

All listed prices exclude 18% GST. Every invoice shows the base price and GST clearly.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes — from your account settings, any time. Your plan stays active until the end of the billing period, and you are not charged for the next cycle.

What happens after the 14-day trial?

Add a payment method to continue on the paid plan, or your account drops to Free. Either way, your data stays intact.

How is the counter leak check worked out?

It multiplies three things you enter. Scheme money: units sold × share on a scheme × average payout × share of claims that slip. Staff time: hours a day × 26 days × Rs 150 an hour. Overdue khaata: the amount × 1.5% a month, roughly what that money costs to borrow. It is an estimate from your own figures, not a promise of savings.
